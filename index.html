Become a TMGM Introducing Broker

An Introducing Broker (IB) refers new traders to TMGM and earns ongoing commission on their trading activity. You don't need to manage funds or provide financial advice — you're the connector.

If you already talk to people about markets, investing, or trading — as a content creator, community lead, or just the person friends ask for advice — this turns that trust into a structured partnership.
